HM-B0 objective:
Extend the staged-construction lifecycle to HydroMechanics without changing the mechanical A4L semantics.

Required semantics:
1. Construction substeps must not advance physical time.
2. Deactivation removes both mechanical and hydraulic/storage/flux contributions of the inactive domain consistently.
3. Reactivation/backfill restores both displacement and liquid-pressure operators for the new domain.
4. Mechanical birth configuration uses the last converged displacement as stress-free reference unless an explicit placement state overrides it.
5. Hydraulic placement state is explicit and multiphysics-ready; liquid pressure and related storage state must never be inherited accidentally from the pre-excavation material.
6. Fresh MFront/MGIS constitutive state remains mandatory on material reassignment/reactivation.
7. Full physical operator at birth; no artificial stiffness-to-zero homotopy.
8. Global construction disequilibrium is solved adaptively at constant physical time.
9. Deterministic E2E must cover active HM material -> inactive/void -> reactivated/backfill HM domain and verify displacement, liquid pressure, hydraulic/storage contribution removal/restoration, and no stale constitutive state.
10. Literature-first debugging: before introducing new numerical machinery, check established staged-construction/element-birth treatment in Abaqus, PLAXIS, FLAC3D and peer-reviewed coupled HM literature.
